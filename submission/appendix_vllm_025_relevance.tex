\subsection{Current-vLLM Relevance Audit}
\label{app:vllm025}

We additionally audit the structural premise on an unmodified official
vLLM~0.25.1 wheel.  We replay five RULER and five LongBench HotpotQA cases under
native APC, comparing raw order against the same frozen qualified layout across
three fresh server processes (60 measured requests).  This is a relevance and
portability audit, not a claim that the vLLM~0.10.2 PSTP patch applies unchanged:
online certification and transactional publication are disabled in this cell.

The compiled layout reduces mean TTFT by 79.84\% on RULER and 96.77\% on the
long-context HotpotQA audit, while reducing observed new-prefill tokens by
94.20\% and 99.53\%, respectively.  Completion is 100\%, every paired
raw/compiled output is identical, and task-score deltas are zero.  RULER exact
retrieval passes all cases; the identical 60\% HotpotQA pass rate is used only
as paired behavior evidence, not as an official LongBench claim.

Source inspection finds the V1 admission, prefix lookup, allocation, full-block
registration, and partial-block registration objects, and all audited files
match the installed wheel manifest.  Native lookup still exposes a longest
prefix chain rather than arbitrary non-contiguous segment reuse.  The required
object classes therefore remain available, but changed cache-publication
interfaces require a version-specific PSTP adaptation before a full
vLLM~0.25.1 port can be claimed.
