\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
ContractKV turns a compiled KV prefix into an evidence-bound service object. CALC exposes dependency-valid stable prefixes, scoped certification determines admissible use, PSTP controls atomic visibility, and template identities let authorized requests share qualification and physical state. Source-disjoint, task-preserving experiments show 1.619$\times$, 1.109$\times$, and 1.070$\times$ full-cost throughput with unchanged output IDs in all 1,296 admitted compiled future pairs. Publication controls and lifecycle traces explain how those gains arise and when they diminish. The service-level result is a measured route from repeated prompt content to qualified, transactionally controlled reuse.
