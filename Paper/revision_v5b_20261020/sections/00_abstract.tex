\begin{abstract}
In multi-tenant LLM services, repeated documents often follow changing questions or observations, leaving substantial reusable content beyond the reach of native prefix caching. Reordering that content creates a physical prefix, but its behavior and shared visibility must be governed together. We present ContractKV, which makes a dependency-valid compiled prefix an evidence-bound service object. CALC exposes legal stable prefixes; execution-scoped token-path and termination checks govern behavioral admission; Prefix-Selective Transactional Promotion (PSTP) atomically publishes eligible complete KV blocks; and Service-Template Certificates bind authorized reuse to a versioned lifecycle and amortize qualification. On six source-disjoint HotpotQA/RepoBench templates with task-preserving 16K inputs and zero tool slack, Qwen3-8B, Mistral-7B and Llama-3.1-8B achieve 1.619$\times$, 1.109$\times$ and 1.070$\times$ complete-service throughput. All 1,296 admitted compiled future pairs reproduce raw output token IDs under greedy decoding. In deployment-template controls, sharing qualification reduces verifier calls from 125 to five and unhidden verification exposure from 25.05 to 3.24 ms/request. At a 25-tenant, 80\%-load deployment point, 500-ms SLO goodput rises from 0.173 to 0.831 requests/s. Together, these results demonstrate how scoped admission, atomic publication and template-level cost sharing turn structural repetition into useful shared service state.
\end{abstract}
