\section{All-Route Physical Authorization}
\label{sec:s-authorization}
The all-route integration is a distinct correctness regression, not a rerun of the performance figures. A trusted authority validates membership and active epoch, then HMAC-binds engine, policy, template, domain and epoch to a request identity. All raw, L0/fallback, verifier and compiled dispatches recheck identity; native cache salt is incorporated into the first block hash and propagates through its parent-hash chain. An expired request is denied before backend submission. A current identity holding an incompatible compiled binding uses its own authorized raw namespace.

The frozen matrix has three models, three fresh-process repetitions and seven contexts per cell (six real tasks and one synthetic marker). All 828 business requests complete, and actual prompt IDs match the frozen tokenizer results. With each engine/cache retained throughout, legacy cross-domain and old-binding/new-epoch raw requests each hit the earlier namespace in 63/63 controls. Under the repaired identity, both first cross-domain and first new-epoch requests have zero hit tokens in all 63 controls. Same-domain/epoch own repeats, a second authorized principal, and each new domain/epoch's own repeats all retain hits (63/63 each). Actual block hashes and marker coverage are logged. The controls share seven contexts across repeated models/processes.

There are 135 qualification decisions, 75 actual verifier RPCs and 93 rejected/abstained decisions with zero newly published candidate tokens. All 63 stale request identities are blocked before dispatch. The 42 admitted free-running pairs comprise 24 real-task and 18 marker pairs; their output IDs and finish states agree. The 24 real-task compiled requests hit the complete committed prefix. Mistral admits no real-task candidate in this matrix. No performance ratio is formed between its unequal legacy and repaired phases. Operator semantic mislabels and authorized-domain timing inference remain separate trust boundaries.

\label{sec:s-fusion-residency}

It retains exact Raw/candidate admission, the original margin/EOS floors and PSTP, while separately probing fused execution evidence and revocable certified-prefix priority in the native eviction queue.
