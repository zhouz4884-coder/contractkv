\section{Exploratory Exact Raw Behavior Scope}
\label{sec:s-strict-exploration}
After freezing the primary evaluation, we separately probed how an exact Raw-witness behavioral gate changes deployment-template economics. \emph{This is exploratory evidence and is not part of the primary query-qualified or deployment-qualified service estimates.} The existing deployment policy attests a representative compiled trajectory and authorizes later metadata-varying requests within the declared schema; such a registration does not establish exact Raw equivalence. In a separate 27-cell deployment study (10,368 successful business requests), 3,456 compiled strategy--Raw pairs showed 1,178 output-ID/finish differences and 36 $>0.03$ task-score decreases. The decreases repeat six distinct tenant/turn conditions in one Mistral HotpotQA source across three processes and both qualification granularities; all outcomes remain archived. They are not falsely treated as 36 independent source failures.

The exploratory successor binds an actual Raw witness, exact Raw and candidate prompt IDs, model/runtime and generation settings, and an independently checked candidate stable-prefix hash to a strict request-level admission record. An unseen exact input stays raw; successful candidate free-generation, unchanged output IDs/stop, the original Raw/Candidate/EOS margins, and PSTP are required. It does not relax qualification floors or repurpose the compiled deployment witness as a Raw witness.

The strict successor compiles 56/128 Qwen and 28/128 Mistral requests; its 84 compiled strategy--Raw executions (not 84 independent task sources) show zero output-ID/EOS changes and zero score drops. The deployment comparator compiles 96/128 and 64/128 and retains output divergences. The stricter route reduces eligible coverage and adds controller work: Qwen saves 53.462 s of business HTTP time but spends 36.467 s in registration plus 5.775 s other added work (net saving 11.220 s); Mistral saves 14.608 s but spends 19.070 s plus 2.132 s (net loss 6.594 s). All 954 generated HTTP and 34 verifier HTTP requests across development and independent cells are accounted for in the repository.

A native cache audit finds 42 full and 42 zero hits among 84 strictly compiled requests; every zero hit is the first tenant of a repeated source pair, while the immediately following second tenant hits the legal prefix. That eight-access experiment has no pinning/eviction counterfactual. A later, separately bounded residency/fusion study is reported below. This bounded exercise demonstrates correct exact-scope enforcement at a significant economic cost. It cannot be combined with the original deployment Goodput to assert strict zero-degradation and high Goodput simultaneously, and it does not establish equivalence for arbitrary new queries or observations. Source-level raw evidence, failed conditions, actual identities and complete fees are archived in \url{https://github.com/zhouz4884-coder/contractkv/tree/main/results/tsc_behavior_scope_repair_20261020}.
